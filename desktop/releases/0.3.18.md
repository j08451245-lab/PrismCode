# Signed on your Mac

## macOS

- **PrismCode signs itself on your Mac.** The app is signed with a certificate created once on your machine and kept in its own keychain, without any prompt. It happens on install and at startup, so keychain access and privacy permissions survive a reinstall instead of being asked for again.

## Known limitations

- The first local signing changes the app's identity once, so macOS may ask one more time for keychain access to secrets PrismCode saved before.
- Local signing does not satisfy Gatekeeper; the first launch from a downloaded DMG still shows the usual warning.
